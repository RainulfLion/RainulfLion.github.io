# Westeros castle collection

[Browse the gallery](preview.html) · [Open the map generator](../../index.html) · [Prompt catalog](catalog.json)

24 strongholds and one east-west ice Wall, with prominent silhouettes and transparent backgrounds. Each original is a 1024 × 1536 PNG in [../map-ready/](../map-ready/). The online gallery uses smaller WebP copies and provides full-resolution PNG downloads.

Choose **Westeros** in the generator's paint palette. Castles also appear in the Stamp menu with their proper names. Paint Wall tiles in a horizontal row to extend it east to west. Alignment loads automatically; for local-file PNG export, load the map-ready folder once.

Castle Black joins the Wall on both sides. Eastwatch joins from the west and ends at the sea to its east. These three tiles share one alignment frame so their Wall crests stay level.

The collection includes Winterfell, Dragonstone, Storm's End, Casterly Rock, Highgarden, Riverrun, the Eyrie, Pyke, Sunspear, Harrenhal, the Red Keep, Castle Black, Eastwatch-by-the-Sea, the Twins, Moat Cailin, Greywater Watch, Last Hearth, the Dreadfort, Karhold, Castle Cerwyn, Deepwood Motte, Horn Hill, Brightwater Keep, Oldtown's Hightower, and the Wall.

All art was made with built-in image generation using the project's Winterfell tile as its style reference. The catalog preserves the prompts and generated artifact identifiers. These are artistic interpretations.
