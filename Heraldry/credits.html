THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE ART

Artists & source credits

This catalog includes 24 illustrations from the Book of Traceable Heraldic Art, compiled by Mathghamhain Ua Ruadháin, alongside the supplied project artwork and the designer’s built-in symbols.

The imported illustrations and their transparent PNG exports are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Credit the named artists and sources and share adaptations under the same terms. Read the source collection’s terms. The artists do not endorse this catalog.

The supplied PNGs and built-in designer vectors retain their original terms. No additional reuse license was supplied for those files. Individual entries identify which terms apply.

The supplied designer was inspired by the DrawShield.net Guide to Blazonry by Karl R. Wilcox (2020). The reference poster is not redistributed here.
